# 🎨 Creator Onboarding Guide

**Last Updated: March 15, 2026**

## Welcome, Creator!

This guide walks you through becoming a creator on AUDHDITIES—someone who can list products, earn money, and build a following.

## Prerequisites

- ✅ A registered account
- ✅ Verified email address
- ✅ Completed profile (avatar + bio)

## Step 1: Apply for Creator Status

1. Log in to your dashboard
2. Click "Become a Creator" in the sidebar
3. Fill out the application:
   - **Portfolio URL**: Link to your work
   - **Creative categories**: Writing, art, code, etc.
   - **Description**: Tell us about yourself
   - **Residual percentage**: Choose your platform fee (0-50%)

4. Submit for review

## Step 2: Wait for Approval

Applications are reviewed within 1-3 business days. You'll receive an email when approved.

**While waiting:** Prepare your first product!

## Step 3: Set Up Payments (Stripe Connect)

Once approved:

1. Go to Creator Dashboard → Payments
2. Click "Connect Stripe Account"
3. Follow Stripe's onboarding (takes 5-10 minutes)
4. Verify your identity and bank details

Stripe handles all the complex financial compliance. We never see your banking info.

## Step 4: Create Your First Product

1. Go to Creator Dashboard → Products
2. Click "New Product"
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive
   - **Description**: What is it?
   - **Price tiers**: Community (discount), Ally (standard), Corporate (premium)
   - **Residual pool**: What % of your earnings go to contributors?
   - **Media**: Images, previews, downloads

4. Save as draft or publish immediately

## Step 5: Add Contributors (Optional)

If others helped create this product:

1. Edit your product
2. Go to "Contributors" tab
3. Add by username or email
4. Assign percentage of residual pool
5. They'll be notified and must accept

## Step 6: Publish and Promote

1. Set product to "Published"
2. Share on your social channels
3. Add to your profile gallery
4. Consider a launch discount

## Earnings Breakdown

When your product sells:

Sale Price: $100
├── Platform Fee (your chosen %): $20
├── Your Immediate Payment: $56
└── Contributor Pool: $24
├── Contributor A: $12
└── Contributor B: $12


You control the platform fee (0-50%). Higher fees mean more for contributors but less immediate payment.

## Tips for Success

- **Start with one product** and learn the system
- **Price reasonably**—you can always raise later
- **Engage with buyers** in comments/DMs
- **Update products** regularly
- **Add contributors** generously—it builds community
