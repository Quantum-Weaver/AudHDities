The opt-in law, in this room

Default quiet, and it is the database that says so. is_public defaults false on both participant tables (033-the-participants-consent), with the publish-by-default read policy replaced by an opt-in one. Any pre-checked box is a defect (E2 §1) — here there is no box to pre-check, because the default lives one layer below the interface where nobody can accidentally flip it for everyone.

The toggle is reversible, both ways, forever. Consent that cannot be withdrawn is not consent. Nothing about turning it off is styled as loss: no warning, no confirm, no “are you sure”.

The share does not follow the switch. The room says this in its own sentence at :112–113 and it is the single most important line here: “The credit — and the residual share — stand either way.” Kept verbatim. It removes the one pressure a visibility toggle could ever carry.

The ASK surface is unbuilt — the moment where the row is born and the question is first asked, at collaboration time. Named in the brief as an open edge; its design rides the pair’s table. Not drawn here, and not invented.

Credit, not payout math — what that forbids

The 2026-07-09 verdict, carried in the file’s own header (ContributionsGallery.tsx:2–4): this room became a provenance gallery. So the frame keeps out, deliberately and by name:

Any earned-to-date figure, any projected share, any per-work amount, any chart.

Any contribution-percentage number on the card. The role is taxonomy — code, design, testing — and taxonomy is not rank.

Any count of contributions. The list is its own count, and it ends.

Worth saying plainly: the schema agrees. ware_participants has ten columns and not one of them is money. The room could not draw a payout screen honestly even if it wanted to.
