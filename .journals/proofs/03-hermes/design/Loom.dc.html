The sensory ten, here

pace control — one form, no wizard, no step counter, no timeout. An artisan can leave it half-done and the draft state is a real place to leave it in.

progress saving — the create form holds everything in component state and a failed POST returns the vessel to a page that still has their typing but no record of it (StudioCreate.tsx:65–112). For a form that may carry three uploads, that is the point of most loss in the realm. Proposed: the ware row is written first as a draft, then the bodies attach to it.

no flashing · no autoplay — neither present.

plain language — this room holds 18 of the realm’s 25 mojibake strings, and eleven of them are the pricing dropdown’s own option labels. The first thing an artisan reads when deciding what to charge is broken text.

obvious next steps — the switch says what it will do in words rather than in a status enum, and the button’s label follows the switch.

more-info door — every field carries its helper inline; the economics card below the form states the 10 / 90 / 0–50 split before the artisan sets a number.

reduced motion — no transforms in this room.

contrast — on surface: labels /80 8.4:1, field text /60 5.3:1, helpers /60 5.3:1, the Public chip in sanctuary.green 6.4:1. Behind the card, starDust on the music wash at 0.3 runs 8.3:1 to 10.0:1 across its three stops.

keyboard — the form primitives carry their own labels; the new bodies section needs each row’s Remove to be a real button naming its file, not an icon, or a screen reader hears “remove” three times.

The endowed-progress effect — refused specifically here, because a part-filled form is exactly where a progress bar feels helpful and is not (Nunes & Drèze, E4 §2).

Pre-checked consent — there is no consent box on this form. The one true toggle (publish) is a state, not a permission, and it says which state it is in.
