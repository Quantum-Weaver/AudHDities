The four proposed edges, each closing a dead end

the stall → /bazaar/checkout. Taking the Exchange off the hub grid (board ①) leaves that route with no inbound link anywhere in the realm — the hub card at BazaarHub.tsx:56 is its only one. Its content is genuinely good (how exchanges work, the whole split) and it is a more-info door, so it belongs where the question is actually asked: a quiet link at the stall, beside the split. The route lives, and it is reached at the moment it means something.

the stall → the weaver. A ware’s page shows the work and never says who made it. The maker’s room exists, and nothing points at it. E4 §2’s shopkeeper exemplar is a person at a counter; a stall with no person is the thing this realm refused. One line, under the plate.

the Loom → my works. /bazaar/studio/[id] has exactly one inbound, and it is a redirect after saving a draft (StudioCreate.tsx:100). An artisan who saves a draft and closes the tab cannot ever find it again except by remembering a UUID. The Loom’s landing should list the vessel’s own works, draft and published, with the new-work action on it. That is frame, not feature: without it, half of the Loom is unreachable.

the stall → RECEIVE, for a work already kept. The delivery must be re-askable, or the frame has built a one-shot handover with an expiring link at the end of it. The stall is the natural place: it already knows the ware and the vessel. Board ⑥ step 4.

One edge that should not exist

router.back() is not a way. Three rooms end with a Back button that calls it — CreationDetail.tsx:153, CreatorDetail.tsx:154, VendorDetail.tsx:139. For a vessel who arrived by a pasted link, or from a search engine, or from the delivery mail, that button leaves the Sanctuary. Every one of those three pages already carries a named return at the top. Drawn with the named return only: one way back, and it always lands somewhere real.

The empty-state voice, stated once

“waiting for stars to align rather than missing data”

L1-08, via E2 §3 (bus L526–530). Written for mnemosyne, and it is the rule every empty state in this realm was already following before anyone wrote it down. The four sentences the realm already owns — awaits its first threads · preparing their looms · the guild is forming · the maker may weave more — are all the same move: a present-tense state of becoming, with no date and no apology.

Never a promise the base cannot keep. “may weave more”, not “will” — the schema holds no restock signal.

Never a count of what is missing. No “0 works”, no “nothing found (0)”.

Always one thing to do, when there is one. Empty bazaar → the Loom. Empty filter → clear it. Signed out → the door. Where there is genuinely nothing to do, it says nothing rather than inventing an action.

Never a skeleton standing in for an answer. The galleries render six pulsing card-shapes while loading (CreationsGallery.tsx:81 · CreatorsGallery.tsx:46 · VendorsGallery.tsx:46 · ContributionsGallery.tsx:103). When the answer is none, six ghosts have already promised six things. Drawn: the skeleton for the fetch, then the honest sentence — never the skeleton as the resting state.
