The tiers — one ware row per rung

Drawn as three wares rows, one per rung — each with its own price, its own Stripe Price, its own residual_pool_percent = 50, shown together at the stall as one ladder. The alternative — one ware carrying several prices — is drawn and set down on page 2.

The base holds one price per row. wares.price is a single nullable number and pricing_model a single enum (database.types.ts:6561–6562). A rung is a different offer at a different price, not another body of the same offer — so it is a different row, and KP’s one ware per app carrying all its bodies ruling is left exactly as it stands.

The pledge is per row, and it is 50 on each. wares.residual_pool_percent belongs to the ware, so three rows carry three 50s — KP’s own standing setting on their own wares, unchanged by the rung.

Nothing in the base says recurring yet. pricing_model is free · fixed · pay_what_you_want · patronage_only (database.types.ts:6876, and the runtime list at :7143) — no subscription value, and no interval column on wares at all. ware_type is physical · digital · service (:6914), and service is the honest fit for a rung today. The whole gap is named on board ⑥.

The rungs are ruled, and they come from the door being retired. KP ⚛, 2026-08-24: “use the same tiers from the “donate” for the subscription tiers (or 3 of the set) names and levels were fine”, then all five as the set stands. The set is domains/hephaestus/donations/DonationTiers.tsx:15–21: Supporter $10 🕯️ · Guardian $25 🛡️ · Steward $50 🌿 · Visionary $100 🔮 · Sovereign $250 👑. Names, amounts and icons carry over; the descriptions do not — those were the platform speaking about itself, and one of them says creator, a word this canvas has just retired.

The unused enum is now a thing to retire, not a thing to obey. subscription_tier = community · ally · council · corporate (database.types.ts:6905, list at :7174) is a four-name ladder no column anywhere uses, and it is not the ruled set. Named for the DRAFT SQL as a drop, so two ladders never stand in one base.

The tiers are not a platform product. They are one artisan’s recurring ware at one artisan’s stall, which is why they are drawn on this board and not on a page of their own. The platform takes its 10% from them exactly as from a $3.33 compass, and 30% of that returns to the pool exactly as from a $3.33 compass.

The bodies, and the desktop law

KP ⚛, ruled 2026-08-24: one works row + one wares row per app carrying all its bodies · a file_registry row per body · APK + MSI + NSIS on the stall, the AAB for Play.

Three bodies, not a “download” button. Every figure above is read from the shelf, not invented: resonance-assets/releases-current/compass/bundle/ holds the .apk (132 MB), the -setup.exe (21 MB) and the .msi (23 MB). The version v2.3.6 is read from the bundle’s own filename, never typed.

The fourth body is withheld, and the stall never mentions it. The .aab (95 MB) sits in the same folder and is Play’s upload artifact, not a person’s download (PLAN §5, reading RELEASE-STRUCTURE.md). A stall that lists a file nobody can open is a stall telling a small lie.

Desktop is never a footnote. RELEASE-STRUCTURE.md via PLAN §5: both desktop bundles are release artifacts — “many people cannot use a phone for this”. So mobile and desktop are three equal rows in one list, same height, same weight, in the order the shelf holds them. No “also available for”.

Today the bodies are a sentence, not a list. CreationDetail.tsx:106–110 renders “Available for Android · PC” from metadata.formats (:41–49) — a display string with no file behind it. Neither wares (23 cols) nor works (17) holds a file pointer; cover_url and media_urls are display images. The frame replaces the sentence with the rows, and board ④ is where they are put there.
