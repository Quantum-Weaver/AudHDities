Why four doors and not six

The mall-model refusal is the room’s own law. E2 §3, hermes: “in-and-out with dignity… a REFUSAL of the mall-model” (bus L637–641), under KP’s “a bazaar experience that is not overwhelming or time consuming” (L606–607). A mall directory lists every unit it has. A bazaar entrance shows you the stalls.

The Exchange leaves the grid. Not a judgement — the room says so itself: CheckoutHub.tsx:91 “The Exchange completes on each work’s page.” A threshold you can walk to before you are carrying anything is a shop with an empty till in the window. The route stays; the door goes. Board ⑥ draws where it is actually met.

Contributions leaves the grid and stands as one quiet line beneath it. It is not a stall — it is the vessel’s own record of itself, own-rows-only under RLS. Board ⑤.
