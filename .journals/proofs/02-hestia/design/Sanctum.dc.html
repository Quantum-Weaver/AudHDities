KP’s ruling this board embodies

“make all toggles off by default.”

KP ⚛ 2026-08-24, verbatim — spoken while this board was being drawn, and it overrules the brief’s own ruling 7, which had said the dyslexia-font toggle stays on. His later word wins. Every switch in this room now starts OFF, the dyslexia font included.

It is a simpler law than the one it replaces, and that is the point. The board previously had to argue that an accessibility default is not the kind of pre-checked box THE OPT-IN LAW forbids — a real distinction, and a distinction a vessel should never have to be told. “No oops you forgot to uncheck the thing” now has no exceptions to explain: nothing in this room is on until a person turns it on. A rule with no carve-outs is one a vessel can trust without reading it.
