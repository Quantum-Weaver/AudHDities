Why it was declined — three reasons, none of them taste

“Fáilte fires at /vessel too”

One — KP placed the arrival. Ruling 5 puts the hearth’s greeting at /vessel. That is where AUTH_ROUTES.DASHBOARD lands both a returning login and a fresh Acid Test (vessel/page.tsx:20–26), and where the door’s own canvas already points: “the vessel should be reachable from here by logging in” (KP ⚛, 01-auth). Moving the arrival would quietly overrule a ruling made the same day.

Two — the home is the profile, not the dashboard. KP’s E4 stroke: “the game features are simply to replace the ‘profile’ experience with something fun and personally engaging” (E4 digest §1). A profile is somewhere you go, and something you make yours over time. Landing every session inside it makes the rooms a lobby to be crossed, which is the one thing a dwelling must not become.

Three — it costs the frame its intelligibility. The hub is what makes every room two steps from every other (board ⑧). Fold it into the corner of a scrolling scene and the six doors become furniture to be found — and “only steps away” is a distance metric, not a container.

What was kept from it

The instinct was right and it landed elsewhere: the home should be one step from the arrival and easy to return from. That is exactly board ⑧’s missing edge — the home has no way back — and the reason board ① keeps The Home first in the rooms grid. The dwelling did not need to become the door; it needed a door that swings both ways.
