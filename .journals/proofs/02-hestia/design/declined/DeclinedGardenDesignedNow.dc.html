Why it was declined — KP’s word the same day

“i magine building the framework for each of the next realms before the features within is a good approach, as some features are cross referenced realm-wise. the game side of the vessel experience, not as vital as a functional bazaar, athena with contents, etc”

KP ⚛ 2026-08-24, verbatim, spelling kept. He names the game side of the vessel by name and puts it after the frame. The garden, the collections and the companions are that side.

And a second reason, from the ground

Designing these now would be designing against empty tables. vessel_interiors, vessel_decorations, garden_plots and plant_stages are BUILT schema holding no rows (E2 digest §2), and KP sequenced the seeding himself: “we are going to seed the database with assets for the experiences and collections discussed and designed” … “after we design the front” (E2 §1, L1950–1954). Drawing a plot that shows “Moonpetal” invents a seed catalog that is his to seed.

The companion is the sharpest case. KP’s ruling corrects the whole research direction: “companion clues is supposed to be a gentle reminder about the vessels actual pets in life” (E4 §1). A companion is a person’s real animal, remembered. There is no honest way to draw someone else’s pet, and a designed placeholder would be exactly the invented-creature attachment his ruling exists to prevent.

What was kept from it

The places. Board ② draws all four as reserved ground with their real empty-state copy quoted verbatim, so the frame shows where they land without pretending to have landed them. That is what a marked second pass is for: a space held open is information; a space filled in early is a decision taken from him.
