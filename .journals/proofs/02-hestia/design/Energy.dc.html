The room the list cannot reach

There is no door to the entry. /vessel/energy/[id] is a real route with a real page, a real fetch and a real delete — and EnergyLog.tsx:370–415 renders each entry as a bare <Card> with no Link around it. Nothing anywhere in the app links to it (searched: zero hits outside the route itself). The only way to delete an energy entry is to type its id into the address bar. Drawn as a link, which is all it needs.

And no way to write the note it displays. The detail renders entry.notes (:86) and the list previews it (:404); handleCreate sends notes: data.notes || null (:193) — but the form has only two fields (:297–318) and neither is notes. The field can be shown and can never be filled. Drawn: an optional note, one line of permission under it.

Anti-scarcity — the sharpest case in the realm

The rhythm card draws a person’s body as a filling bar. :341–347: Progress value={average * 10} max={100}, under “4.7 / 10 average” in 3xl neurospark. A bar has a full state, so a tired week renders as a nearly-empty one. That is a completion meter (E2 §6 forbids them by name) pointed at how a neurodivergent person feels — which is the one thing in this app that must never have a target.

Drawn: the bar and the /10 go, the sentence stays. The insight text is already the good half and is kept to the word — “This is not failure — it is a signal to rest” is the anti-deficit law spoken plainly (E2 §6 forbids deficit-model framing). The average becomes its own name from the room’s own ladder: 4.7 reads “Steady”, using ENERGY_LABELS (:43–54) which already exist. A word cannot be full or empty.

The ten-point scale itself is honest. Every step is a named feeling — Drained, Low, Tired, Dim, Steady, Alright, Good, Bright, Glowing, Radiant — not a grade. “Drained” is a true thing to be, and the colour ramp for 1–2 is void.base grey, not red. Kept whole.

The day-of-week reading is a question, not a verdict. :132 ends “Would you like to plan rest for those days?” — it hands the pattern back rather than prescribing from it. Kept verbatim; this is what pattern-reading should sound like.

No streak. Nothing counts consecutive days, and the empty state does not ask for one.
