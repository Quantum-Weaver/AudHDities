DECLINED Velkomin as a gate

Velkomin.

Enter

a full-bleed overlay; the page waits behind it until it is dismissed

What it was

The door’s word as a full-screen threshold at /sanctuary — the page held back until the visitor stepped through. It made the crossing unmistakable, and that was the whole appeal.

Why it was declined

1 — the component’s own law forbids it. VelkominGreeting.tsx:33–34: “A soft opacity fade only; instant under prefers-reduced-motion.” A gate is not a fade, it is an interruption.

2 — it fails pace control. A visitor who arrives is made to perform an action before they are allowed to read. The sensory ten’s first point is the person setting the pace, not the page.

3 — KP asked for a word, not a door before the door. His stroke is “Velkomin at the door + Fáilte at the Hearth” — a greeting spoken as you come in, which is what board ① draws.
