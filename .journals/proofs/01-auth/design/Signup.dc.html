The laws standing on this board

THE OPT-IN LAW. “no oops you forgot to uncheck the thing” — any pre-checked box is a defect (E2 digest §1). The census counted it: accept_terms, SignupForm.tsx:126–130, not pre-checked, 0 defects (Part 8). Drawn empty, and it stays empty.
