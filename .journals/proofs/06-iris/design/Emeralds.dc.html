The room says today that Received “awaits a proper endpoint.” That was honest and it is now out of date. An emerald already records the thing it praised; that thing already records who made it. Received is those two facts read in one breath.

So what is owed is a door, not a table — nothing is stored twice, nothing new is invented, and the Observatory keeps ownership of emeralds exactly as it does now.

The two tiles are gone

The room as it stands opens with two big numbers — Sparks Given and Sparks Received — one of them a live count of your own generosity and the other a permanent dash. A number beside your kindness turns a gift into a tally, which is the shape this house refuses everywhere else.

The list says everything the tile said, and says it about the emerald rather than about you.

Waiting, not missing

Both halves get their own quiet state, and each names where the act happens — an emerald is given from wherever the thing stands, not from this room. The empty room teaches the gesture.
