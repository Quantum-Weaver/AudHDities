Neither door is a new place to store anything. The letter already records who wrote it and who it was handed to, and both of those are vessels with faces of their own — the doors only read what is already written down.

The letters come back

Today the room sends a letter, thanks you, and then forgets it in front of you. The thread page exists and nothing in the app links to it — you would have to know the address to reach your own words.

The list of your letters closes that. Only your own: this table is shared with the Forge’s contact form, and someone else’s letter is not this room’s to show.

An answer with a person behind it

An answer signed by nobody reads like an automatic reply, which is the last thing the Healing Flame should sound like. A name makes the answer a person and gives the reader someone to have been helped by.

Urgency is not a promise

The room asks how soon you need help, and the sent screen answers “within 24 hours.” That number is a promise nothing in the house can keep. The waiting letter above says only that nobody has answered yet, which is true at every hour.

Waiting, not missing

An unanswered letter is marked waiting, not overdue and not failed. The crisis card stands whatever else is on the page, and never moves below the fold.
