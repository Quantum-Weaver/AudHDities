What the re-point fixes

Both rooms today ask a neighbouring realm for channels. That door has never existed, in any version of this house, so both rooms have always shown nothing and blamed the emptiness on the community. The table they wanted is iris’s own, and it has been standing there the whole time.

Two things the board refuses to draw

Subscriber counts. The room as it stands prints a number of subscribers on every card. The base holds no such number, and nothing anywhere counts members — so every one of those numbers is a zero dressed as a measurement.

A subscribe button that only changes its own mind. Today the button flips a word on the screen and remembers nothing. A control that does nothing is a small deception, so it is not drawn until there is somewhere for the joining to live.

Waiting, not missing

The channel with no words says exactly which part is built and which part is not, in one plain sentence, without apologising and without blaming the reader for a quiet room.
