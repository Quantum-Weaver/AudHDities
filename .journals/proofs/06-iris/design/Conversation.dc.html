The send, addressed properly

The room as it stands sends every message to the person writing it — a conversation talking only to itself. The address of the room you are standing in is the person you are writing to, so the recipient is read from the room and a message addressed to yourself is refused with a plain sentence.

Read means read

Opening the conversation is the reading, so opening it is what marks the words read. Nothing is reported back to the person who wrote them. The mark exists only so your own unread count is true.

Reactions are not drawn

There is nothing in the base to hold one — no table, no column, nothing. Drawing a row of little faces would be drawing a feature out of air, which is the one thing this canvas refuses.

The shape is settled for the day it is built: a reaction is a row, one per person, never a number on a badge. That way taking one back is a different act from adding one, and a room can always say who felt what. The refusal is written down on the second page.

Waiting, not missing

A conversation with no words is not broken. It says so plainly and leaves the composer open, because the cure is one sentence away and belongs to the reader.
