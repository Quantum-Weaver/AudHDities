Two things that leave

The send button. Today it takes an address and a message, does nothing with either, and says “Sent!” No mail is sent by anything in this house. A button that lies about having helped someone is the worst kind on a page about welcome.

The Sent Invitations list. A permanent empty box for a thing that was never recorded — it reads as a failure of yours rather than an absence of ours.

Two rooms, one truth

The first drawing is the room once the invitation road is laid; the second is the same room today, with the road named as unbuilt and the one address that really works handed over instead. Neither hands the reader a link that goes nowhere. The copy link today points at a road that has never existed anywhere in this house.

Whose word this is

Waiting, not missing
