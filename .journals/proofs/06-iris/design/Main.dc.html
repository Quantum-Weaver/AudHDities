What changes here

The hub today reads nothing at all — seven cards and a covenant, and no idea whether anyone has spoken to you. The waiting area is the only new thing on this board, and it is a gathering of what the tables already hold.

It carries no numbers about you. It says who, and what, and which room the thing is in. A count of your own life is a score, and this house does not keep one.

The names

Every person named on this board is named by their vessel’s own display name, taken from their own profile. That is the only place in this house a name can honestly come from.

The one thing still to be created is a permission: today a vessel may read their own face and no one else’s. Until that opens, a name the Sanctuary cannot reach is said plainly and never replaced with an invention.

Waiting, not missing

The quiet state is drawn at the same size and in the same frame as the full one. It is a room in a state, not a hole where a room should be — and the seven doors do not move or dim when nothing is waiting.
