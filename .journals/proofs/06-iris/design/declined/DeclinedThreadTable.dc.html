boards ② and ③ · declined by the house crystal

What was drawn, and set down

A real conversation: its own row, its own name, its two vessels, its own last-spoken time, a proper unread count kept up to date. Every message points at it. The Stream becomes a plain list of rows rather than a gathering done in the moment.

a conversation, as a stored thing

who is in it · when it last moved · how many words are unread · whether it is hidden

Why it was declined — the house crystal, in KP’s own frame

What is needed already exists. Refraction, not manufacture.

And in the commission itself: “assuming backend things exist that are needed (we will create them and not be cuplicating things)” — KP ⚛ 2026-08-31, verbatim, spelling kept.

And why the ruling is right, in the realm’s own terms

The pair already is the conversation. Every message in this house records who spoke and who was spoken to. Those two facts together name the conversation exactly, with no ambiguity and no second place for it to be wrong. A table would be a second copy of something the messages already say.

And a copy is a thing that can disagree with itself. A stored unread count drifts from the messages it counts; a stored last-moved time drifts from the last word. Every one of those drifts is a room telling a vessel something untrue about their own life.

The teaching this realm inherited is the sharper version: which word I answer and which conversation I am in are two facts and must never be collapsed into one. A conversations table does not honour that — it makes the second fact into a thing, and then the first has to be squeezed in beside it.

What was kept from it

The address. The idea was right that a conversation needs a name you can walk to. It got one without a table: the address of a conversation is the other person. That also cures the send bug for free, because the room you are standing in now says who the message is for — and a message to yourself is refused rather than stored.
