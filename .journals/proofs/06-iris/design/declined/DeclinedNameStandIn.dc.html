Why it was declined — the commission says so in one line

“please complete iris, if names do not exist in the flow, put them in the flow, the names come from the vessels display name, there is zero other logical places to derive it from”

KP ⚛ 2026-08-31, verbatim, spelling kept. Zero other logical places settles it: a stand-in is another place, and a prettier one, which is exactly what makes it dangerous.

And why the ruling is right, in the realm’s own terms

The placeholder that lives in the room today is not a bug — it is a well-meant stand-in that hardened into a lie. Every conversation partner in the Sanctuary is currently the same two words. Replacing one stand-in with a nicer stand-in repeats exactly the mistake at a higher polish.

And a stand-in is unfalsifiable. If two rows both say “A Sanctuary Soul,” a reader cannot tell whether they are two people or one, whether the wall is closed or the profile is empty, or whether anything is wrong at all. “Name not yet reachable” is longer and less charming and it is the only version a reader can act on.

It also hides the one thing that would fix it. The cure is a single permission, drafted and waiting for KP’s hand. A friendly mask over that would make a solvable problem invisible for another year.

What was kept from it

The warmth. The honest row is not an error state: no red, no warning triangle, no broken-avatar glyph. It is a dashed edge, a plain sentence, and the word yet — which is the whole difference between a wall and a delay.
