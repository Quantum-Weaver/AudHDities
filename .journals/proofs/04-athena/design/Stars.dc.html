What this room does for you

It stops at five hundred points in a day. That number is yours — move it, or set it to nothing at all; a rest day is a boundary too.

It stops at a hundred pops in an hour, whatever the day’s number says.

After a quarter of an hour of unbroken play it pauses the room itself and asks you to breathe.

And at fifty pops it says the same thing once, gently, and lets you carry on.

Every figure is the built one: DEFAULT_DAILY_MAX 500 :66 · DEFAULT_HOURLY_MAX 100 :67 · 15 min :246 · 50 pops :301. Nothing invented; nothing rounded. The room may state its extent — that is E4’s own line, “the interface may state extent at the ask, never the remainder.”

Reduced motion, drawn proposed

The stars stop drifting and start arriving. Under prefers-reduced-motion the animation loop keeps its spawn rhythm but not its travel: a star fades in where it belongs, waits its while, and fades out. Same game, same rarities, same caps — no motion across the screen.

The pop mark stops pinging. animate-ping becomes a still glyph that fades. And hover:scale-110 active:scale-90 get the guard the rest of the house already writes.

KP ruled the guard mended; he did not rule its shape. This is the plainest shape that keeps the game a game. HANDOFF.md already holds the principle for the whole app: “motion is content, so it needs consent.”

What the game is FOR

“the game features are simply to replace the ‘profile’ experience with something fun and personally engaging.”

KP ⚛, E4 digest §1, verbatim. This is the sentence that makes every deletion above obvious: a profile page is a place you look at yourself, not a place you are measured. The realm law, signed thrice

“The Floating Stars game guards against its own compulsion loop — nothing built or redrawn in this realm may undo that. Attention returned, never harvested.” (athena)/README.md:111–113, echoed at REALM-BUS.md:118–123 and countersigned by the reimagining seat. This board removes only measurement, never a guard. All four caps survive intact; two of them get louder.

The sensory ten, here

pace control — the realm’s strongest row. Pause and resume by hand (:379), a forced pause at fifteen minutes, a breath at fifty pops, and a ceiling the vessel writes themselves and carries to every device.

progress saving — every pop is written at once (POST vessel_bubbles :288); the session score is deliberately ephemeral. Nothing is lost, nothing is hoarded.

no flashing — animate-ping :426 on every pop, unguarded. Thirty pops a minute is thirty pings. Mended above.

no autoplay — no sound, no video. The stars drift on their own but nothing plays at you.

plain language — “Take a breath” is the plainest sentence in the app. Two failing lines rewritten, below.

obvious next steps — every stop screen offers exactly two doors: carry on, or go back to the gallery.

more-info door — the new caps panel IS this row. Today the room enforces four rules and explains none of them until you hit one.

reduced motion — the realm’s worst row and its most-needed mend. A requestAnimationFrame loop (:230–254) moves up to thirty objects continuously, plus hover:scale-110 active:scale-90 :405, plus the ping. Not one motion-reduce anywhere in the file. ruled: mend

contrast — the subtitle and the header ratio at /40 read 3.18:1; the breath message and “You’ve collected…” at /50 read 4.32:1; “Daily progress” and the slider’s own best line at /30 read 2.30:1. Measured. Drawn at /70 and /82.

keyboard — each star IS a real button with an aria-label (:400–415), which is better than most games manage — but focus:outline-none at :405 removes the focus ring and nothing replaces it. A keyboard walker can reach every star and cannot see which one they are on. Proposed: a visible focus ring, since removing one is never the mend.

The opt-in law, here

No toggles in this room; the boundary is a slider that loads the vessel’s own row first and falls back to the charter’s gentle number, never to a generous one (BubbleLimitSlider.tsx:34–45). Under KP’s ⚛ “make all toggles off by default”, that is the right shape already: the permissive setting is never the default.
