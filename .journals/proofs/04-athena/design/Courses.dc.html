🚪 Velkomin

The door’s word, and why it is said once per crossing.

Old Norse — welcome. The door’s own word, said once per crossing: every arrival is greeted, and no arrival is interrogated.

The greeting law is small but load-bearing: you are welcomed for existing, not for what you do here. There is no onboarding gauntlet, no engagement streak waiting to be broken, no guilt at the threshold when you have been away.

The stars will still be here when you return. So will the door, and so will the word.
