The ground this board is drawn on

E4’s synthesis, “Quests as invitations / environmental storytelling”: BOTW’s sighting model — you see a thing on the horizon and go, or you do not. Nothing is assigned, nothing is tracked, and the world does not notice either way. That is what this hall already is in its writing, and what its one button is not.

“Begin Quest” — the plainest true thing

Today it is a lie of omission. QuestDetail.tsx:99 renders a primary button with no onClick. A vessel presses it, nothing happens, and the room has told them they failed at something. That is worse than having no button.

Drawn: the button goes and the objectives become the doors. Five of the six seeded quests spell their steps as places — “Visit the Path”, “Open the Archive”, “Open your journal”. So the honest “begin” is the first door, already on the page. Nothing starts, because a quest here is not a session; it is a suggestion you can take now.

Why not wire a real Begin? Because submission machinery is one of the three 🚩 flags, held for the second pass at KP’s word. Drawing a button that would work later is designing the held thing. This draws what is true today and stays out of that sitting’s way. Proposed: no ruling names it.

And the seam holds either way. Two of the six doors leave this realm entirely — “Visit your garden” and “Open your journal” land in hestia. Drawn as ordinary doors, because they are.

What is held, and named

🚩 quest submission machinery — one of the trio (with completion_points math and sigil award triggers) that KP held for the second pass this sitting. Nothing on this board wires it, proposes a schema for it, or draws a state that assumes it. The room works without it: six invitations, twenty-four doors, no ledger.

no flashing · no autoplay — nothing moves in this hall at all.
