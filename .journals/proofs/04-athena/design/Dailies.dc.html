Why a closed door earns a board

Because the realm brief calls the dailies the unbuilt half of Athena, and a canvas that draws six full halls and says nothing about the seventh reads as though the seventh were forgotten. It is not forgotten. It is reserved — and the difference between those two things is the entire reason this board is here and empty.

The open seam, named — not decided

Where the words come from is a real fork and it is KP’s: the Grammar’s atoms and molecules through the bridge, or a Superposition table of their own. The realm bus names both and picks neither ((athena)/REALM-BUS.md:88–90, 150–157). It is also shared with iris’s Voice doorway, which is why the keeper’s own counsel was to convene it once rather than three times. This canvas takes no position.

The one thing this board does assert
