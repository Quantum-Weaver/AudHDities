The sensory ten, here

pace control — the hub is a fixed catalog of four sections that ends. Nothing loads on scroll, nothing reorders, nothing expires.

progress saving — the hub holds no state; the footer holds none. Nothing on this board can lose a reader’s work.

no flashing — nothing blinks. No “new” badge on the App Privacy card: it is new to the house, not new to the reader, and marking it would be a nudge.

no autoplay — nothing plays anywhere in this realm.

plain language — the footer’s six are the plainest words available: Privacy · App Privacy · Terms · Transparency · Press · Contact. Deliberately not the house names (The Covenant, The Agreement, The Scroll) — a footer is where a stranger goes when they need the boring true thing, and the poetry lives one click in.

obvious next steps — every card on the hub states its destination in words; every footer link is a noun a person can already spell.

more-info door — the hub is the realm’s more-info door, and after this pass every card on it points at a page that exists.

reduced motion — stills to nothing. The hub’s card hovers become tint-only; nothing scales.

contrast — measured. Hub card titles starDust on surface 12.3:1; the footer at opacity-80 over the footer’s own ground 9.4:1. The badges as they stand read 6.2 to 9.7:1 in Tailwind stock — legible, but not the house palette, which is the finding rather than the failure.

keyboard — the hub is a plain list of links in reading order; the footer is six more. One card leaves the tab order and one joins it, so the count does not change.
