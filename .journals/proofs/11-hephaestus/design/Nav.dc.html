The bar should be derived, not re-typed

Today the bar is a second hand-kept list (Navigation.tsx:34–41) beside the street’s own (the-street.ts:42), and the street file exists because that duplication drifted once already: “so the NAV and the LÉARSCÁIL read the same ground and cannot drift apart” (the-street.ts:5–8). The four items are four hrefs that already live in the street; drawing them from it is one line of code and one class of bug gone.

After the Playground ruling, three of the four are the street’s own realm doors — /vessel (The Hearth), /bazaar (The Bazaar), /environments (The Realms). The fourth, /sanctuary, is a room inside the Forge group (the-street.ts:135) rather than that realm’s door, because it is the visitors’ front door and the Forge’s own door is /forge. So a derived bar reads four hrefs from the street; it does not read four realms.

2 · Vessel signed out goes to /login.

“yes”

KP ⚛ 2026-08-24, verbatim, on the drawn lean.

The label stays “Vessel” in both states and the href becomes /login when signed out. Four fixed items in a fixed order, whatever your state — the geometry law is that a place does not move (the-street.ts:10–13), and a door that renames itself depending on who is looking is a door you cannot learn.

Not chosen, and recorded: pointing signed-out Vessel at /sanctuary, which would make two of the four items the same destination on a visitor’s first screen; and hiding the item when signed out, which shrinks the bar to three and teaches the wrong geometry.

The sensory ten, here

pace control — the bar is static and fixed. Ten controls become five; nothing on it loads, polls, badges, or changes while you look at it.

progress saving — the bar holds no state of its own; the drawer and map both close on navigation (Navigation.tsx:71–74) so nothing is left half-open behind you.

no flashing — nothing blinks. No notification dot, no pulse, no attention-seeking badge on any of the four.

no autoplay — nothing plays. The map does not open itself, ever.

plain language — four house words instead of nine. Vessel · Bazaar · Playground · Sanctuary are all named in KP’s own sentence; the two that are not obvious on sight (Vessel, Sanctuary) are the two a reader meets first anyway.

obvious next steps — this is the board’s whole argument. Nine near-identical items is a directory; four is a way in. The Map is where the directory belongs, and it says so on its own foot: “Every door stays where you left it.” (Navigation.tsx:235).

more-info door — the Map, unchanged: the LÉARSCÁIL drawn, then every realm and every real room beneath it (:200–233). Esc folds it (:79–81).

reduced motion — already guarded on every transition in the file, and kept. Stills to nothing; nothing on the bar scales or slides.

contrast — measured on the bar’s ground. Resting label at /50 4.3:1, hover at /80 9.4:1, active neurospark on its own /15 tint 8.0:1, the focus ring in hearth-gold 12.7:1. Resting /50 is the weak one — it clears 4.5:1 only because the item is 14px semibold; the secondary row’s /40 (:131) reads 3.2:1 at 12px and is one of the things that leaves with it.

keyboard — four links, then Map, then the auth link: six stops instead of eleven. The Map is a real button with aria-haspopup and aria-expanded already (:144–145); focus returns to it when Esc folds the map.

The refusal column (E4 §6), checked on this board

The Gruen transfer — refused. Four items, all visible at once, all one tap deep, in a fixed order that never shuffles. No item is placed to be passed on the way to another.

Badges · counts · streaks — none on any item, none added. A nav that counts at you is a nav that decides your next move for you.

Metaphor never obscures the exit — the sovereignty rider, already in the street as alwaysOpen on the Hearth (the-street.ts:33–38,48). The way home is never a puzzle, and on this bar it is the first item.

Hover-only affordances — refused. Every one of the four states its own name at rest; nothing appears only under a mouse.
