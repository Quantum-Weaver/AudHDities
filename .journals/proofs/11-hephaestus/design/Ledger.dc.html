The sensory ten, here

pace control — one fetch on mount, no polling, no live ticker. Money that updates while you are reading it is money you cannot check. “Show earlier” is a button, never an infinite scroll.

progress saving — the filter is the only state; it belongs in the URL so a councillor can send a colleague the exact view they are looking at.

no flashing — five pulsing skeletons today (above). Drawn still.

no autoplay — nothing plays, nothing counts up. A number that animates from zero to its value is a number you have to wait for.

plain language — the kind labels are already the house’s own translation of the enum (:24–30: sale → “exchange”, residual_payout → “residual distribution”) and are kept verbatim. The new column header is four words of English: what it hangs from.

obvious next steps — two, both at the top: back to the Council, up to the public telling. A working page should say where the finished version of itself lives.

more-info door — the link up is the more-info door, and it is the reason no prose needs repeating here.

reduced motion — stills to nothing. No skeleton pulse, no row entrance, no filter transition beyond an instant colour change.

contrast — measured on surface #1A1F35: heading starDust 12.3:1, row text at /78 8.0:1, the date in mono at /68 6.4:1, the exchange amount in neurospark 9.0:1. Today’s dates are the failure: :143 sets them at /30, which reads 2.3:1 — the one field on a ledger that must be legible, drawn fainter than anything else on the row.

keyboard — two links, then six filter chips as real buttons, then the rows. The rows are not links (nothing to open yet) so they take no tab stop and do not pretend to.
