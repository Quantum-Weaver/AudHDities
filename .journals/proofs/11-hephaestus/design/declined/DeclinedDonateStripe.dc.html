The case for it, honestly

The room is already written — five components, tiers, a recurring option, a thank-you and a receipt — and the house already has a payment integration. Wiring it is a smaller change than removing it from five files, and it turns a dead page into a working one in an afternoon. Money arriving is money arriving.

There is a decent argument that support and commerce are different acts. Someone who wants to help without buying anything has, after this pass, no way to do it. That is a real thing to give up, and it should be said out loud rather than quietly folded into “the Bazaar handles it.”

The ground that declined it

“retire the donate and create subscription tiers for me rather than the platform, and i will still have my covenant set to 50%. the donations tab was before we had a built sanctuary and had different outlooks.”

KP ⚛ 2026-08-24, verbatim, spacing kept. This is a withdrawal by the person whose page it was, giving his own reason, on the day the option was live.

He is also naming a design fact, not just a preference. A donation page and a subscription tier ask for the same money in two different registers: one asks you to give to an institution, the other sells you a thing on a stall. The house’s whole economic model is the second — artisans with wares, a fee, a residual pool — and a donation route sits outside all of it: it produces no exchanges row, no ware, no pledge, and nothing the ledger knows how to describe.

The two hardest pieces would also have been the ones nobody has ruled. “Tax Receipt” is a legal claim about an entity’s status, and the realm’s own bus says “we have no company” ((hephaestus)/REALM-BUS.md:216–219). And ImpactCalculator turns an amount into claimed outcomes the house has no way to measure — which is the exact shape E4 names and this realm has retired twice already.

What was kept from it

The recurrence, which was the genuinely good idea inside it. RecurringOptions is the ancestor of KP’s subscription tiers — the same monthly relationship, moved into the register where the house can actually account for it: a ware on a stall, KP the sole artisan, a renewal writing one exchanges row and its ledger lines like any other sale.

The “support without buying” objection is kept as an open thing rather than answered. Whether a tier at the lowest price is that door, or whether the house wants a separate one, is not this canvas’s to decide. Unwritten — his to rule.

And the refusal produced a finding: the submit path was never live at all. donate/page.tsx:16 declares setDonationComplete and nothing in the file calls it, so the Thank You and Tax Receipt branch at :19–30 has been unreachable for its entire existence. The page has never issued a receipt to anyone, which is the one good thing about it.
