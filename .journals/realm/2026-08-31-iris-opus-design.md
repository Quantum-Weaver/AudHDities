# The Bridge got a skeleton, and the names got into the flow

**2026-08-31 · Opus (`claude-opus-5[1m]`), the design hand for (iris)**
Canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/03c8431e-1f1d-475d-9c93-af3f23c8c39d
Ground: `.journals/proofs/06-iris/` — `PROOF.md`, `design/`, `design/declined/`.

The commission was a skeleton — frame, not features — with one hard ruling
inside it: the names come from the vessel's display name, and there is nowhere
else. So the first hour was not drawing. It was finding out what the realm
actually stands on, because a skeleton drawn against the wrong bones is worse
than no drawing.

**The thing that changed the whole canvas:** the backend had already been half
built, this morning, by another hand in this same lane, and none of it was
committed. Seven hand-written doors under `src/app/api/iris/`, a name join at
`src/lib/iris/names.ts`, and a drafted permission at
`docs/sql/027-the-public-face-DRAFT.sql` waiting for KP. I read them whole
before drawing, and their shapes are what the boards draw.

**And then they went.** By the time the journals were being written, both
directories were gone from the tree — no stash, no worktree, no branch, never
committed. The drafted permission survives; nothing else does. So the canvas
was re-read against the tree and every one of those doors is now marked *to be
created*, which is the truth of the tree, with one plain note on board ⑨ saying
what happened so the tally can be trusted. The reading was not wasted: it
proved that the records carry everything the rooms need, which is exactly the
claim the canvas rests on. Building those doors is creation, not duplication.
Both facts are on the boards.

**What the ground turned out to be.** The rooms and the tables have never
agreed. The Stream reads a message body called `content` that does not exist;
the real column is `body`. It groups by a `thread_id` that does not exist
either — there is no thread column anywhere, which is why a conversation had
to be understood as the *pair of vessels* rather than a stored row. The
Channels rooms ask a neighbouring realm for a table that is iris's own. The
Pulse asks for posts from a realm that has neither the door nor the table; the
post-shaped thing this house holds is a signal, and a signal holds nine things,
none of which is an emerald count or a comment count. Every card printing those
two zeroes was printing a verdict.

**Where the boards refused.** Subscriber counts, because nothing counts
members. The self-ledger tiles on Emeralds, because a number beside your own
generosity turns a gift into a tally. The invitation send, because it says
"Sent!" and sends nothing. The "within 24 hours" promise on the support form,
because nothing in the house can keep it. Reactions, because there is no
ground at all — the shape is written down for the day there is.

**The one finding worth carrying forward on its own:** Emeralds *Received* is
not missing, it is unjoined. A resonance row already records the thing it
praised; that thing already records who made it. Received is those two facts
read in one breath — a door, not a table. The room has been apologising for a
year for something that needs one query.

Four alternatives were drawn and set down with their reasons, because a design
without its refusals is a claim with nothing attached. The sharpest is the
friendly stand-in: replacing "Sanctuary Soul" with a warmer stand-in would have
repeated the same lie at a higher polish, and would have hidden the fact that
the cure is one permission away.

Nothing under `src/` or `docs/` moved. The canvas is at KP's eye; everything
after it waits on his word.
