---

Fourteen files changed, two deleted. The canvas Mantel drew was approved whole,
so almost nothing needed deciding — the work was reading eight boards carefully
enough to build exactly what they drew and nothing beside it.

**What the rooms became.** `/vessel` is six rooms in one grid, three across,
with the Sanctum standing among them as a place rather than an action, and the
Constellation reachable without opening a menu. Bubbles left the grid; it comes
back as a button under the rooms only when a vessel turns it on. Fáilte stands
above the name, and stands down on a crossing so the door's word is never
doubled. `/vessel/home` has a way out — the room a vessel is meant to dwell in
was the only one with no exit. The Sanctum's switches are all off and all
controlled; the dyslexia one was uncontrolled, so turning it on and coming back
showed it off while the base had it right. The Scroll's delete says what it does
in words a person does not have to translate. The energy log's rhythm card no
longer draws a tired week as a nearly-empty bar, and its entry page — a real
route with a real delete that nothing in the app linked to — has a door now.

**The one thing I got wrong and caught.** I unified seven sign-in empty states
when the board unified six: `SceneRenderer`'s "Enter the Sanctuary to stand in
your home." was the sentence the other six were being changed *to match*, not
one of the six. I noticed it in the first screenshot, which is exactly what the
screenshots are for. Restored, rebuilt, re-shot.

**Left standing, deliberately.** The garden, keepsakes, decorations, companions
and the hanging are untouched second-pass places. Echoes is a named place in the
entry with nothing drawn into it. The council house is not a star because
nothing in the schema says which house a vessel is in — a dark sky is not an
incomplete sky, and inventing a line would have been the exact failure the board
refuses. `bubble_vessel_button` has no column yet; it is written defensively and
read defensively, the way the ceremony switches waited for theirs.

**And one thing reversed mid-build.** I retired the `/1000` bar in the status
bar under E2 gate ④, then KP ruled the opposite: *"the score in the status bar
exists only to be replaced when we discover what we will use the channel for."*
Restored, byte-clean, before the meters ran. The gate wanted an empty slot; he
wants a held one. Held.

Meters: `npm run type-check` 0 errors, `npm run build` exit 0. Seven screenshots
in `.journals/proofs/02-hestia/build/`, all signed-out — the base holds no
vessels, and a session I faked would be a picture I faked.
