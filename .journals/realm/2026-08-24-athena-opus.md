## What I found that the briefs had not

Three things came out of reading the source rather than the reports.

**The realm's most-repeated defect is contrast, and it lands on its best
sentences.** `text-star-dust/40` measures 3.18:1 and it carries every gallery
subtitle in the realm. `/30` is 2.30:1 and carries "0 — a rest day is a
boundary too." `/35` is 2.69:1 and carries the provenance line — the one
place in the whole app that renders a citation for a reader, in a house whose
ground file says *provenance on every claim*. The law is kept and then set at
eleven pixels in the faintest grey available. Every figure measured this
sitting; the method reproduces `HANDOFF.md`'s own 14.4:1 and 12.3:1 exactly.

**Three routes stand in the wrong weather.** `/library/bubbles`, `/bubbles/play`
and `/bubbles/[slug]` have no row in `PAGE_ENVIRONMENT_MAP` and fall through
to `lounge`. The heaviest room in the realm wears another realm's affect, and
nothing in the code would ever say so.

**The back-link discipline is complete.** Thirteen rooms below the hub,
thirteen doors back, every one in the same voice with the definite article
(the hub needs none, being where the header nav lands). The Hearth's
canvas had to add one. This realm needed none. Worth writing down, because a
thing done right everywhere is invisible.

## What I judged

The census flagged "collect" five times and "progress" once. Three of the
five *collect* hits are honest and stayed — "Tap bubbles to collect them" is
a true instruction, "Part of {collection}" is taxonomy. Two failed, and
neither failed for the verb: "Collect bubbles and **earn points**" is
pointsification, and "You've collected {n} points today. **Come back tomorrow
for more stars!**" is the appointment mechanic, refused by name. The single
*progress* hit left by KP's ruling along with both n/N displays and both bars.

Six rewrites out of 1,739 lines. This realm writes better than it is given
credit for, and thirteen of its fourteen empty states are already right in
the hard way — they say *being woven*, *being written*, *not yet*, and never
*missing*.

## What I would not draw

The dailies. KP ruled that door his own, and the temptation on a canvas is to
leave one small honest sketch behind. Board ⑦ is a closed door with the
ground that sitting inherits and nothing else — no route, no mechanic, no
placeholder. Reserving a room means leaving it empty, including of good ideas.

The three 🚩 flags likewise. `Begin Quest` has no handler, and the plainest
true thing was to retire the button rather than draw one that *would* work
later — because five of the six seeded quests already spell their steps as
places, so the doors are the beginning.

## The one honest consequence

Mending the Honors to earned-only makes the room empty for **everyone**,
today, because the award triggers are a held flag. That is honest empty, not
broken empty, and the sentence the room already has covers it exactly. The
board says so plainly rather than designing around it.
