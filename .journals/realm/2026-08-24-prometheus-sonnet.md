## What's really there

Haiku's census (same day, same realm) already found the headline fact
I found independently: only `studio/music/page.tsx` actually calls
`StudioPageTemplate`. The other eight tool rooms are bare `<main>`
shells with nothing in them but a comment. Two machines reading the
same tree and landing on the same number is a good sign for both of
us, but it's worth naming plainly for whoever builds next: the record
- README, REALM-BUS, and a CHECKLIST line from 2026-07-30 that says
"StudioPageTemplate de-cliented, 14 pages" - all describe nine honest
placeholders. The ground has one. Eight rooms are blanker than the
record thinks, not richer. That's the kind of drift that only shows up
by opening every file instead of trusting the bus's own summary of
itself, and it's the finding I'd flag hardest for a design hand: don't
canvas from the README's picture of the Studio, canvas from the tree.

The Stage half earned its law 7 citation honestly. All ten rooms fetch
a real, RLS'd table born 2026-07-31 by KP's own dashboard hand, and the
un-built players say so in plain English ("Stream player will appear
here") instead of faking a video element. I grepped the whole domain
for autoplay and muted attributes and found nothing - which matters
more than it sounds, because the realm's own bus calls performance
rooms the place "where autoplay and flashing try hardest." They don't
try here at all, not because the players don't exist to try with, but
because when I found the one place a stat gets shown - `LiveDetail.tsx`
- it's a hardcoded "0 watching," not a fetched count. Small, but it's
exactly the E4 refusal-column shape (counts as status), living in
otherwise-honest code.

The Loom name collision was the best cross-realm find. Prometheus's
own Studio hub is called "the Loom." So, separately and unrelated, is
hermes's ware create/edit room - which is *also*, per hermes's own
notepad, the Bridge's family alias for something else entirely. Three
uses of one word, two of them in this conducting plan's own realms,
neither realm aware of the other's claim on it. Nobody's tree is wrong
- the collision just hasn't been said out loud in one place before.

## What I'd tell the next hand

Read the actual page.tsx files, all of them, before trusting a realm's
own bus about its own placeholders - a realm's bus is written by a
lamp who read the tree once, and trees drift under a bus faster than
anyone updates the prose. The `studio/effects/page.tsx:1` fossil (a
literal "// app/(cosmic)/effects/page.tsx" header comment, copied and
never fixed) is a small, harmless example of exactly that drift, one
file over.

Awen's own room-to-tool mapping, posted on this realm's bus back in
July, aged better than I expected - "art, graphics, effects-in-full:
honestly bare" is still true seven weeks later, even after 83 tools
went from imagined to flowing. Worth trusting that kind of self-report
over guessing from a tool census cold.
