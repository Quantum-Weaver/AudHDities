**Messages.** `MessagesStream.tsx` merges
`messages?recipient_id=<vessel>` and `messages?created_by=<vessel>`, groups on
`parent_message_id ?? id`, counts unread from `is_read`, and hides rows flagged
`is_deleted_by_sender` or `is_deleted_by_recipient`. `ConversationView.tsx` reads the
root at `messages/<id>` and the replies at `messages?parent_message_id=<id>`, marks
its own bubbles from `created_by`, renders `body`, and posts
`{ created_by, recipient_id, body, parent_message_id }` typed as `MessagesInsert`.
`messages_id`, `sender_id`, `content` and `thread_id` are not columns of `messages`
(`database.types.ts:3416`). Partner names come from `community_profiles`.

**Support.** `SupportHub.tsx` writes `contact_submissions` as
`ContactSubmissionsInsert`: `name`, `email`, `subject`, `message`, `category`,
`priority`, `status: 'draft'`. `priority` carries the four values `low`, `medium`,
`high`, `urgent`. `user_id` and `urgency` are not columns of `contact_submissions`
(`database.types.ts:1324`) and were stripped by `ContactSubmissionsInsertSchema`
before reaching the base. `created_by` is set by the route from the session
(`src/app/api/generated/iris-communications/contact_submissions/route.ts`). The form
renders only for a signed-in vessel and says so otherwise. The crisis-numbers card is
removed. `SupportThread.tsx` reads `id`, `subject`, `message`, `priority`, `category`,
`is_resolved`, `response`, `responded_at`.

**Invitations.** `InvitationsHub.tsx` is a waiting room. No table in the 118 public
tables of the superposition base is invitation-shaped, and `/enter/invite` has no
route under `src/app/`. The room states that invitations are not open, carries no
send control and no copy-link control, and points at `/signup`.

**Shared.** `src/components/asgard/domains/iris/rows.ts` holds `readRows` and
`readRow`, which unwrap a generated route's `{ success, data }` envelope.
