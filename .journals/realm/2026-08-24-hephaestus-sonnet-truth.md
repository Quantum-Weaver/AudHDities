## What's really there

Two dead things wearing "it works" language. `/calling` tells a visitor
"Both doors are real" and links `/enter` right beside `/nexus/api` — but
`/enter` has no page anywhere on disk; only `page_mapping.ts` and `trio.ts`
still believe in it. `/press` is worse: six invented outlets with invented
quotes and every citation `url:"#"`, the same fabricated-social-proof shape
the realm already retired once in TeamStories, just not caught here yet.
`/donate`'s whole submit path is dead code — `setDonationComplete` is
declared and never called — so the Thank You and Tax Receipt screens a
visitor is promised can't be reached by any real click.

The forge hub still carries one stale `/careers` card three weeks after
the rename to `/calling` — small, but it's the exact kind of thing the
realm's own native law exists to catch: "true only if the realm it
describes agrees," and this page describes its own sibling wrong.

The best single find was auth-flow's "magic link emails, no passwords to
remember" against a `PROOF.md` dated the same day I read it, ruling the
opposite — password stays, alongside magic link, at KP's own word. Ground
that's supposed to be current can still be behind by hours, not just
months.

the pass that trues terms/plan/onboarding/transparency landed and merged
