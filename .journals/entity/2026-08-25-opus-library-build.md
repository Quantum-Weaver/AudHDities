*`claude-opus-5[1m]`, sent by Anacrusis at KP's ⚛ word. One sitting, one realm,
the main tree.*

**What I did.** Read the 76 KB spec whole before touching a file, then built
the Library on `refine/athena-2026-08-25`: nine commits, one per room, all three
gates. The score left the hub's door and the pill above it went with it. Begin
Quest — a button that did nothing and told you that you had failed at something
— retired, and the objectives it sat under became the doors they always
described. The game's rose became the curator's magenta, because a rule that
says *no red anywhere* is not a suggestion, and while I was there I took the
last four reds out of the realm. The sidebar stopped doing the vessel's
arithmetic for them and started saying the names of what they found instead. The
caps learned to speak before you hit one. The stars learned to arrive without
travelling, for anyone who asked motion not to move.

**The thing I did not expect.** The spec said 5 collections and 30 stars and no
colour columns. The live base said 123 stars, 13 collections, four columns that
exist, and five duplicate collection rows carrying accents over an empty shelf
while the stars hang off their older twins. I built gate C to read the columns
and it reads them honestly — which today means five collections wearing a colour
and eight wearing none, and no star with stripes at all. It would have been very
easy to hard-code thirteen accents from the app's JSON and take a beautiful
screenshot. The room would have been lying, and it would have kept lying after
the rows changed.

**What I could not do.** Every signed-in walk. There are no vessel credentials
here and making one in KP's live base is a write that is not mine to make. So
the flip, the ×N pill, the collected sieve, the caps panel, the "Found so far"
card and the Honors' earned path are built and compiled and never seen by an
eye. I said so plainly rather than dressing a type-check as a proof.

**What waits.** KP's hand on `025` — and now on the seam it already left in his
base. Fifteen printed silences, none of them guessed at. And one sentence I had
to write myself, for a room that must not tell a vessel they have earned
nothing when the truth is that the read was walled; it is named in the return
so he can strike it.
