## Read before you touch this repo

- **THE REALM IS WEATHER, NOT WALLPAPER.** `EnvironmentLayer.tsx` is the fifth
  instrument on the environment-key spine (Header · StatusBar · ContinuityBeam ·
  navigation · this). Its own header carries the ruling: *"Affect from
  constants, not from pictures"* and *"the old panoramas were photographs behind
  content; the token wash is calmer on purpose — **attention returned, never
  harvested**."* The breathing keeps the image era's measured **47.7s** tempo and
  stills to nothing under reduced motion, because **motion is content, so it
  needs consent.** `PanoramaViewer` and `QuantumBackground` stand retired beside
  it, each annotated with its successor.
- **THE 0.3 WASH IS AN ACCESSIBILITY STRUCTURE, NOT A TASTE.** Measured
  2026-08-18: `starDust` on `deepSpace` is **14.4:1** and on `surface` **12.3:1**,
  while on a domain colour at full strength it runs **1.2:1 to 9.6:1 — mostly
  failing**. So the ground carries the text and the realm hue is weather over it
  at ambient strength. **Text legibility must never depend on which realm you
  are standing in.** Raise `washOpacity` and that guarantee is what you spend.
- **THE PALETTE IS A DEPTH RAMP, AND IT WAS WRITTEN THAT WAY** (KP: *"the cosmic
  colors were written with this in mind"*). Every domain is a triad, and all five
  sampled are **monotonic** `.dark → .base → .light`: quantum .119→.166→.210 ·
  cosmic .165→.221→.279 · fire .222→.283→.354 · library .297→.364→.605 · void
  **.033→.502**. So atmospheric perspective assigns itself — **far = `.dark`,
  mid = `.base`, near = `.light`**, over `deepSpace`, with the blur
  `PARALLAX_LAYERS` already carries (far 2, mid 1, near 0).
  *Two notes for whoever builds the stack:* `library.light` (L=.605) and
  `void.light` (L=.502) lift the ground fastest, so they are where a rising near
  layer will break contrast first. And **void's ramp spans 15× where the others
  span about 2×** — it will read as the deepest space of any realm, which for a
  realm called void is either intent or a good accident.
- **THE STACK IS NAMED AND NOT GROWN.** `EnvironmentLayer` renders **one** wash
  div inside a container classed **`environment-affect-stack`**. KP's design,
  2026-08-18: *"layering in color gradients to be darker from far away, creating
  appearance of 3d as simpler than rendering 3d — for most of the experiences
  that should suffice."* The wash, the souls, the parallax factors and the blurs
  are all authored; the luminance ramp and the stacking are not. **Depth built
  from luminance and blur survives `prefers-reduced-motion`; depth built from
  parallax alone does not** — which is the reason to prefer it here.
- **THE SOULS OUTLIVED THE PICTURES.** `EnvironmentPromptMap` mood/colours/themes
  were *"preserved as data when the panoramas retired"* — **32 environment keys
  onto 11 place-souls**. A realm kept its character when its photograph went.
- **NO ENGINE BELONGS IN THIS REPO.** Weighed 2026-08-18 and written down so it
  is not re-argued: the destinations here are **creative areas** — text, uploads,
  editors, forms — and a canvas discards Radix's twelve accessibility primitives,
  Stripe's iframes and SSR crawlability at once. For a house whose premise is
  neurodivergent dignity, a screen-reader-opaque surface is a contradiction and
  not a tradeoff. *If a genuinely playable thing ever appears, it is embedded on
  its own route — never the frame the site is built inside.*
